ALINITA PARTNERS

We do not simply list real estate; we prepare it for a transaction.

The sale of a high-value hotel or commercial asset requires more than traditional brokerage. It demands a clearly defined investor universe, compelling financial and operational positioning, disciplined confidentiality and a carefully managed transaction process.

Alinita Partners works as a boutique advisory platform connecting asset owners, family offices, institutional investors, hotel groups and strategic buyers across selected opportunities.

WHAT WE DO

Transaction-led advisory

Rather than marketing every asset in the same way, we develop a tailored strategy around the transaction structure, asset profile and target investor universe.

01

Sell-Side Advisory

Preparation and positioning of hotels and large-scale real estate assets, investor materials, buyer targeting, discreet marketing, offer management and transaction support.

02

Buy-Side Search

Targeted sourcing of hotels, resorts, commercial real estate and development opportunities based on defined investment criteria, including off-market situations.

03

Capital & Strategic Introductions

Curated introductions to family offices, real estate investors, operators and strategic partners where there is a clear fit with the opportunity.
